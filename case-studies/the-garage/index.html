Quote Request Tool — a fix for The Garage's broken flow

A real audit of a real local business's site, and a working prototype of the fix. Not affiliated with, built by, or endorsed by The Garage — this is independent portfolio work.

What this is: I looked at garagewichita.com, a real auto shop's real website, found a broken customer-facing flow, and built a working replacement as a demonstration. This page is not live on their site, not sent to their systems, and not something they asked for or have seen. It exists to show how I'd approach a real engineering problem for a real business — nothing here is published, deployed, or represented as official.

What I found

A quick audit of their live site turned up a broken customer path and some maintenance gaps:

  • The "Get a Quote" link leads nowhere documented — no visible form, no clear next step for a customer trying to request an estimate.
  • Booking runs through a disconnected third-party tool (a Mitchell1 CRM page), separate from their own branding and site.
  • No pricing shown anywhere for actual repair services — only the car wash has prices listed.
  • A broken Facebook embed throwing a visible "Error validating access token" message on the homepage.
  • Visible typos in live copy ("Expereince," "memebership," "DoesThe").

The clearest, most fixable gap: a customer trying to request a quote hits a dead end. Below is a working version of what that flow should do.
